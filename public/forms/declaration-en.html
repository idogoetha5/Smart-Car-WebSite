Declaration and undertaking

To Smart Car 2008 Ltd.

  1. I, the undersigned, whose details appear in this form, authorize all charges for the rental of any vehicles that I or anyone acting on my behalf rent or have rented from you during the period specified in this form and shown in the rental documents. These charges are subject to the prices set out in the rental agreement and/or rental invoice and/or your accounting records, and to all rental terms printed on both sides of the agreement and/or appearing on the rental invoice, including charges for additional services and damage to the vehicles.
  2. This undertaking applies to all rental agreements that continue the above agreement, including any replacement vehicles.

    This undertaking also applies to all rentals made by the persons or entities identified in this form, if any. I therefore guarantee all debts those entities owe you, whether under the Guarantee Law or the law of negotiable instruments, for any contractual debt and/or debt under such an instrument, as applicable.

  3. Under this undertaking I authorize you to charge my account for all the above amounts using the payment method associated with the reference stated in this form and/or any other payment method I have provided or will provide to you.
  4. I understand that, in reliance on my authorization and undertaking, you have agreed to rent the vehicle to the renter identified in this form. This undertaking secures your rights and is therefore irrevocable.
  5. By signing below, I fully and unconditionally waive any right granted to an individual guarantor and/or a limited guarantor under the Guarantee Law and/or any amendments already enacted or enacted in the future.
