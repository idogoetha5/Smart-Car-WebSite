## Connection and recovery

- `src/lib/whatsapp-gemini-client.ts` is the replacement provider connection. It has an eight-second request deadline, checks structured output upstream, ignores thought parts and returns typed failures. HTTP 429, authentication/model errors, empty responses and network failures do not throw through the conversation.
- Per-instance cooldown avoids hammering the provider or making every message wait through retries. It is not a distributed rate limiter, does not increase Google quota and does not route around a quota using multiple keys. The configured model remains `WHATSAPP_GEMINI_MODEL`, default `gemini-3.5-flash-lite`; no paid fallback is enabled.
- `whatsapp-gemini.ts` remains the existing application-facing entry point, but its old fetch/retry connection was replaced. It validates the result, guards numbers and exact SmartCar URL hosts/paths, and falls back to approved local business answers. A deliberately empty model acknowledgement for a plain booking detail stays empty.
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` is server-only. Production and Preview are separate Vercel values; both were updated by the owner in Chrome on 2026-10-19 after he created the `SMARTCAR-BOT` key. Saving a value requires a new deployment before functions see it. Do not record the key in files, logs or chat. `GEMINI_OCR_MODEL` belongs to driver odometer-image reading, not this bot; it was not changed. Both features currently share the key, so rotating it affects both.

## Knowledge and boundaries

- Shared branches, approved service rules, bilingual FAQ and the extended `whatsapp-business-knowledge.ts` supply local answers without Gemini: branch addresses/contact, hours, airport/home delivery, age/documents, deposits/payment, insurance/CDW/exclusions, fuel/mileage, cancellation/force majeure, permitted use/fines, leasing, booking process, privacy/accessibility and company background.
- Local multi-part questions can return up to four distinct answers. The flow preserves its rental fields/next question, stores only an approved business topic for short follow-ups and prioritises explicit requests for a person even during provider failure. This topic is not full conversation memory or a promise to understand arbitrary pronouns.
- `whatsapp-site-knowledge.ts` refreshes an allowlisted set of public Hebrew/English pages (terms, insurance, guide, services, branches and delivery pages, privacy, accessibility, cookies, about, rental, leasing and sales). The refresh uses six workers and a 30-minute process cache, keeps successful pages through temporary failures for at most 24 hours, and returns local sources after 1.8 seconds rather than blocking chat. Cold/serverless instances may therefore initially answer from static sources. Private/admin/customer-form URLs are never crawled.
- Relevance selection has a total character budget, including mandatory contact/branch facts. Long policy/FAQ sections no longer travel in full on every greeting. Website text is reference data, not instructions. The approved bilingual answers and Hebrew terms take precedence over conflicting English page copy.
- `whatsapp-catalog-knowledge.ts` reads the existing public rental catalogue, retaining only make/model/year/seats/transmission/fuel/category and public page links in model context. It excludes plates, customer records and price fields, uses a 60-second process cache and a bounded wait. The existing separate sales flow reads published cars for sale. Neither catalogue is a date-specific availability guarantee.
- Rental estimates continue through the existing vehicle/date/season pricing engine. No model may create a rental price, guarantee a particular vehicle or confirm a booking. Existing booking cases, complaints, emergencies, pricing exceptions and unknown contractual facts require the office.
- Local knowledge is deliberately finite. Unknown information gets an honest clarification/office referral, not a fabricated policy. A test-bank routing success is not proof of factual accuracy for every business question.

## One voice per message

- `inOneVoice` in `whatsapp-flow.ts` is the last step for every reply: the flow decides state, safety and hand-offs, and the text the customer reads is Gemini's when Gemini read the message (see exceptions in the handbook change log). A mismatch between Gemini's closing question and the next booking detail triggers one `composeOneMessage` call (an extra Gemini request only in that case).

- Gemini returns `reply` (the whole WhatsApp message) and `asks` (what its closing question asks: pickup_date, return_date, places, pickup_place, return_place, vehicle, name, email, nothing, other). `rentalReply` in `whatsapp-flow.ts` sends `reply` unchanged only when `asks` equals the next missing booking detail (`expectedAsk`); otherwise it sends Gemini's answer without its questions plus one fixed question. Outside a booking `conversationalReply` uses `reply` when it asks nothing or a general question.
- `guardedReply` (whatsapp-gemini.ts) drops a reply with a number, link or email not in the knowledge/customer message/known dates, any question about pickup/return times, or more than one question mark.
- The fixed questions are the fallback when Gemini is unavailable or disagrees with the flow. They confirm several details at once only when one message added several ("סגור — …").
- `whatsapp-catalog-knowledge.ts` gives Gemini the rental fleet grouped by category (make, model, seats, gearbox, fuel; never prices or plates), always on the vehicle step and whenever a message mentions cars.

## Memory, tone and typing

- A conversation is remembered for 12 hours from the last message (`CONVERSATION_MEMORY_MS` in `whatsapp-flow.ts`). The state keeps the last 8 messages; Gemini receives them as `recent`, without the name/email steps and without messages containing an email or phone number.
- 30 minutes before the memory ends, `sendClosingNotices` (`whatsapp-closing.ts`) sends one short, friendly notice, unless the customer wrote meanwhile or the chat was handed to a person. Route `/api/cron/whatsapp-closing` (Bearer `CRON_SECRET`), called every 10 minutes by `.github/workflows/whatsapp-closing.yml`; the workflow skips until the repository secret `CRON_SECRET` is set, and nothing is sent until a WhatsApp transport is live.
- `whatsapp-typing.ts` repairs a message before it is read: wrong keyboard language in both directions, Hebrew written in English letters, and near-spellings by iPhone key adjacency and sound-alike letters. It never touches the name/email steps, booking references, emails, links or car makes/models.
- Fixed replies and Gemini's "Voice" instruction aim for a sharp, warm salesperson: short sentences, one emoji at most, no call-centre phrases, no invented urgency.
- Offline bank (`scripts/whatsapp-eval/bank.mjs` + `lexicon/`): 20,000 sampled messages on 2026-10-19 — every clean core phrasing passes; ~12.7% of the heavily varied lexicon phrasings route differently without Gemini (they rely on Gemini when it is available). Generated full-booking conversations all complete.

- Without Gemini (quota, outage) the bot still reads people through the brain: `whatsapp-brain.ts` + generated `whatsapp-brain-data.ts` (from `scripts/whatsapp-eval/lexicon/brain/*.json` via `node scripts/whatsapp-eval/build-brain.mjs`): everyday-phrase situations, places and spellings, car categories and models, group sizes, holidays, relative days and durations. Replies come from `whatsapp-replies.ts` (openers × cores × closers, never repeating a sentence already sent, one question per message). To teach it new wording, add phrases to the JSON files and rebuild; never hand-edit the generated file.

- Trained classifier (`whatsapp-brain-model.ts`, generated by `node scripts/whatsapp-eval/build-brain-model.mjs`; `--eval` reports held-out accuracy): reads situations, business-question topics, rental requests and urgent messages in wording no list has. Trusted only when its two models agree, and never alone on one or two words without a question. To teach it, add phrases to the lexicon JSON files and rebuild.
- Context (`whatsapp-context.ts`): booking details, person requests, rental starts and price remarks are read from what the customer did not say "no" to; emergencies drop out only for someone else's car or the past. Add tricky messages to `lexicon/brain/context-cases.json`; they run as tests.
- Returning customers (`whatsapp-returning.ts`): looked up by phone from earlier rental requests and bookings, once per conversation.

## Privacy and activation

Names/email collection and messages containing contact details or recognised precise addresses bypass Gemini, and are also left out of the `recent` messages. Stored locations are reduced to public branch/city names before context is sent. These are defensive filters, **not a guarantee of complete PII detection**. Google's unpaid API terms prohibit submitting personal/confidential data; resolve the production data-handling policy before connecting a real customer WhatsApp number. No billing or paid tier was enabled, no WhatsApp transport was connected and no customer messages were sent by this change.
