## Why this is not a false pass

Finding 75 exists because a previous run recorded a browser that never
started as a page with zero issues. Two things rule that out here:

1. Every one of the 34 pages returned a result. A page that could not be
   scanned appears as an `error` entry, and there are none.
2. A control run on `/he` was taken with the tag filter removed. It reported
   **649 elements inspected across 27 rules**, and did surface a finding —
   `region` (moderate) — so the harness demonstrably still detects things.
   `region` is an axe best-practice rule and is not part of WCAG 2.1 AA, so
   it does not appear in the filtered result above.

## The one result that needed a human

axe returned 13 `color-contrast` nodes as *incomplete* — "could not
determine", which is not a pass:

- **12** are decorative glyphs (`→` in the language toggle, `▼` on the FAQ
  buttons). axe defers on "content contains only non-text characters". All
  are `aria-hidden` or sit inside a control that carries its own label, so
  1.4.3 does not apply to them.
- **1** is the "Join us for a ride" heading, deferred because its background
  "partially overlaps other elements". Resolved by measurement: no image
  actually intersects the text box, so the effective background is the page
  white. `rgb(182,73,22)` on white is **5.31:1** at 60px/700 — large text
  needs 3:1, and this clears the 4.5:1 normal-text bar as well.

## Manual review, same day

A hands-on pass was run alongside the automated scan, covering what axe
cannot reach. Method: every interactive element on `/he/contact` and on a
vehicle booking page was enumerated in the live DOM and its accessible name
resolved the way an assistive technology would (aria-label, aria-labelledby,
associated `<label>`, `<img alt>` inside a link, then text content).

Results:

- **58 focusable elements on `/he/contact`, none without an accessible
  name.** The one flagged initially — the header logo link — resolved
  correctly through its `<img alt>`; that was a defect in the audit script,
  not in the page.
- **Every form control on both pages is associated with a real `<label
  for=...>`**, not merely placed next to text.
- **SC 1.3.5 Identify Input Purpose failed, and was fixed.** The name, email
  and phone fields on the contact form, the booking form, the leasing form
  and the newsletter carried no `autocomplete` token, so a browser or
  assistive tool could not fill them from stored details. This is an AA
  criterion added in WCAG 2.1 and is not one axe reports — it is exactly the
  class of failure a manual pass exists to find. `name`, `email` and `tel`
  tokens added at every site.
- The one-time-code field in the customer portal already carried
  `autocomplete="one-time-code"` and `inputMode="numeric"`; the tracker's
  note that this was outstanding is out of date.
- Contrast of the floating accessibility control was computed rather than
  eyeballed: **3.94:1** at rest over white, **4.21:1** over the hero band,
  **7.21:1** on hover and keyboard focus, against the 3:1 that SC 1.4.11
  requires.

## What is still NOT established

It evaluated **27 rules**. WCAG 2.1 AA has **50 success criteria**, and
automated tooling can only reach part of them. Untested here, and only
testable by hand:

- 1.3.1 / 1.3.2 — relationships and meaningful sequence
- 1.4.10 reflow at 320px, and 200% text resize
- 1.4.13 — content on hover or focus
- 2.4.3 focus order, 2.4.6 quality of headings and labels
- 3.2.x — consistent navigation and identification
- 3.3.1–3.3.4 — error identification, suggestion and prevention on the
  booking and contact forms
- A screen-reader walkthrough with NVDA or VoiceOver. The pass above reads
  the accessibility tree programmatically, which catches missing and wrong
  names but not how a real screen reader sequences and announces a page.

The admin area is not in the URL list — it is staff-only and behind
authentication.

**A clean automated scan is evidence, not a conformance statement.** A
declaration of conformance with IS 5568 is a legal act and belongs to a
certified accessibility reviewer (מורשה נגישות שירות), not to this file.
